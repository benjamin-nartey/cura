# DEV-GOV-2026-001 — Proposed development-only exception

Status: **PROPOSED**. This amendment is not in force. No person has signed it. It does not accept Phase 02, and it does not authorize Phase 03.

## Authority check

`release-constitution-2026.10` says a change records a version, a reason, and an author role. That is a record of change control. It is not permission to waive a phase gate.

`tools/constitution/validate-decision.mjs` rejects an accepted decision unless the architecture, security, and clinical reviewer roles each have a name and a signature time. The product owner is not one of those roles. The constitution does not allow one person to sign more than one of them.

`charter-2026.10` was approved by Benjamin Tetteh Nartey, product owner, on 2026-10-13T13:26:00Z. That approval covers the charter only.

Phase 03 of the specification says later phases depend on signed-off acceptance evidence from earlier phases. Phase 02 `human_authorization_to_start_next` is null. Reviews P02-R01 through P02-R06 are `PENDING`.

A development-only exception can sit beside a pending Phase 02 acceptance only as a separate, signed decision. It cannot be created by editing a status flag, and it cannot be inferred from a request to continue engineering.

## Proposed decision

While this exception is active, engineering may implement Phase 03 against fictional synthetic data. Phase 02 remains `IN_PROGRESS`. Production clinical break-glass stays disabled. Governance acceptance and production clinical authorization remain separate and ungranted.

## Required signatures before activation

An accepted decision record for this amendment needs all of the following, bound to the hash of this file:

- an architecture reviewer name and signature time
- a security reviewer name and signature time
- a clinical reviewer name and signature time
- a product-owner statement that authorizes this development exception and does not accept Phase 02

The same person cannot fill more than one of the three reviewer roles. The product owner cannot fill any of them. Synthetic workforce names cannot sign.

## Scope, if later activated

- Phase 03 feature work, in existing feature order, using synthetic data.
- Local development and automated tests only.
- Effective until the earliest of: a Phase 02 governance decision, a reviewer rejection of this exception, or the next scheduled review named in the signed record.

## Preserved restrictions

Production and staging clinical break-glass stay disabled. Clinical roles stay unapproved for real use. Emergency elevation cannot open a patient clinical record. Phase 03 consent, privacy, and audit work remains the thing being built, not a claim that it is done. No regulatory or clinical authorization is implied.
