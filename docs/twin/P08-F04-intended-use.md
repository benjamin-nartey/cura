# P08-F04 intended use and boundaries

Purpose: local synthetic evaluation of deterministic Patient Twin background verification and repair operations. Intended users are currently authorized treating clinicians and local operators acting within the same live actor/patient/facility/purpose/source-coverage context. Conservative local engineering risk tier C; qualified classification and clinical/privacy/architecture/licensing reviews remain pending.

Inputs are existing authorized immutable clinical/work source versions, F02 build metadata, current identity and operational time. Outputs are background verification status/age/budgets, durable refresh job progress, exact source/build hash references and immutable success checkpoints. Actions enqueue/retry/cancel a derived refresh or append a derived rebuild. No source clinical record is changed by these operations.

Budgets indicate how recently a background source reproduction check completed. They do not assert allergy reassessment, active medication therapy, physiologic observation freshness, absence of disease, diagnostic significance, treatment safety, clinical reconciliation, informed consent or qualified acceptance. Old/future/unknown supplied clinical times remain visible as source metadata. Source mismatch and missing verification are not hidden by recent processing time.

Local executable workers and bounded status observation support real simulations, including correction, source outage, interrupted processing and repair. There is no production scheduling/service-account authorization, independent clinical execution, external notification, automatic signoff or new terminology entitlement. Patient/history services remain the authority during rebuilds. Deployment retention, workforce/service identity, multi-user performance, clinical thresholds and production release require qualified decisions. Formal Phase08 remains NOT_STARTED; production clinical functionality remains disabled. F05 is outside this feature.
