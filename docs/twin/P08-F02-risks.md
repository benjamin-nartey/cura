# P08-F02 risk controls

Conservative risk C; local synthetic materialization only. Qualified reviews remain pending, production clinical functions disabled.

- Clinical overinterpretation: literal recorded statuses/verification retained; entered-in-error/unknown verification not promoted active, drafts not therapies, no inferred care gaps/risk, no clinical reconciliation. Separate records and equal-time candidates remain separate.
- Recency/clock confusion: clinical time for observation selection, source recording for watermark/lag, processing time separately visible. Unknown/future-versus-own-recording observations unprioritized; no recorded-time substitution or clock-dependent state hash. Unknown operational clock/change time cannot establish lag.
- Unauthorized/cross-patient cache: owner/purpose/coverage/tenant/facility binding, complete live source reauthorization and expected-byte/hash reproduction before facts are served. Source inspection binds selected patient/current build/source member; arbitrary cached refs never grant authority.
- Corruption/replay: immutable builds/receipts, semantic request hash, source-validated append repair, stale replay with facts withheld, atomic rollback and real competing-process tests. Retention limits preserve evidence rather than silent deletion.
- Outage/empty: actual F01 source availability including empty work portfolio is required; unavailable is not healthy-empty. Cancel/offline/context clears facts; reconnect explicit, no write replay/storage queue.
- Deployment gaps: bounded synchronous local rebuild and inherited 500+500 inventory bounds are explicit. Production retention/erasure, performance/SLO, wider source families, clinical uncertainty and automated refresh require later features/qualified decisions.
