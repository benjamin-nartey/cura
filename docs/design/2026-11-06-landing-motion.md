## Autoplay follow-up investigation

User reported stalled playback and requested two additional chapters. Reproduction against the previous compiled build confirmed that hovering anywhere in the window cancelled the timer; pressing Play left pointer/focus guards active, producing data-playing=false while the button said Pause. Chapter selection also permanently paused rotation. The regression-before test fails on the original compiled build.

Fix: explicit auto/paused/playing intent, no hover pause, keyboard-visible focus handling with a functional Play override, fresh full-duration timers after chapter selection, and manual reduced-motion opt-in that preserves still transitions. Native modulo uses chapters.length. New scenes illustrate local billing statuses and role/facility/purpose with source history; they do not grant access, process payments or invoke clinical writes. Five-step controls scroll horizontally within narrow layouts and follow the current step without scrolling the page vertically. Spacing was measured at all four widths to eliminate chapter-height shifts.
