# Standards and Framework Matrix

- **Clinical exchange:** HL7 FHIR R4; R5 compatibility; SMART on FHIR
- **Imaging:** DICOM and DICOMweb
- **Terminology:** SNOMED CT, LOINC, ICD, UCUM; national/local code systems
- **Security:** NIST CSF 2.0; ISO/IEC 27001 family; zero-trust architecture; OWASP ASVS/API/GenAI guidance
- **AI risk:** NIST AI RMF + Generative AI Profile; WHO AI-for-health governance
- **Medical software:** Jurisdiction-dependent application of IEC 62304, ISO 14971, IEC 62366-1, IEC 81001-5-1, ISO 13485 and related standards
- **Privacy:** Jurisdiction matrix including GDPR/EHDS, HIPAA where applicable, Ghana Data Protection Act and other local laws
- **Cloud/SRE:** SLO/error budgets, progressive delivery, chaos testing, SBOM/provenance, policy-as-code
