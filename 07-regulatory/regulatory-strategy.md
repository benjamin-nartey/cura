# Regulatory Strategy

CuraSphere uses a capability-by-capability regulatory model. Administrative and workflow SaaS is separated from clinical decision support and from device-like AI functions. Marketing claims must match intended-use dossiers.

For regulated modules, maintain design controls, risk management, software lifecycle evidence, usability/human-factors evidence, cybersecurity documentation, clinical evaluation, model/data documentation, change-control strategy and post-market surveillance as required by jurisdiction.

Jurisdiction packs should be created for US, EU/EEA, UK, Canada, Ghana and each new market before commercial launch. This document is an engineering control framework, not a legal classification opinion.
