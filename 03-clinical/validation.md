# Clinical Validation Framework

Validation is proportional to intended use and risk. Stages: analytical/technical verification, retrospective clinical evaluation, silent/shadow prospective evaluation, limited clinical pilot, monitored production and periodic revalidation.

Metrics must include task-specific performance, false-positive/false-negative consequences, calibration where relevant, subgroup/site performance, missing-data behavior, workflow outcome, clinician override and adverse-event/safety signals.
