# Clinical Safety Framework

## Safety case
Maintain a living safety case for each clinical-impact capability: intended use, foreseeable misuse, hazards, mitigations, verification evidence, residual risk, monitoring and ownership.

## Automation levels
L0 informational display; L1 summarize/organize; L2 recommend with evidence; L3 draft an action requiring approval; L4 execute low-risk action under policy; L5 autonomous clinical decision is prohibited unless separately validated, regulated and explicitly approved.

## Hazard review triggers
New intended use, new population/site, model change, prompt/tool change, data-source change, UI change affecting interpretation, threshold change, new workflow automation, significant drift or safety incident.

## Human factors
Design against automation bias: visually distinguish source facts, derived facts and AI suggestions; show uncertainty and data age; make override/reject simple; avoid alert flooding.
